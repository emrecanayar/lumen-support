Lumen Privacy Policy

How the Lumen macOS app handles your data.

Effective 5 November 2026

This policy describes the Lumen macOS app, published by Emre Can Ayar. Contact: emrecan.ayar@icloud.com.

Data collection

Lumen does not collect or transmit app usage data, screen captures, recordings, microphone audio, recognized text, or settings to the developer. The app has no account, server connection, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDK.

Screen capture and recording

Zoom, Snip, and Record use the macOS Screen Recording permission. Zoom holds a screen capture in memory for the active session. Snip processes a selected image locally. Recording saves an H.264 MP4 file in Movies/Lumen in your home folder.

System audio and microphone

You can optionally include system audio, microphone audio, or both in a recording. The microphone is off by default. Lumen requests macOS microphone permission when you enable it. Audio is written to the local recording file and is not uploaded by Lumen. You can disable audio in the recording controls and revoke microphone or screen recording permission in System Settings.

Images, recognized text, and the clipboard

You can copy a snip to the clipboard, save it as a PNG in Pictures/Lumen, or pin it on the screen. Text recognition runs locally using Apple’s Vision framework. If you choose Copy Text, the recognized text is placed on the clipboard. Lumen does not read unrelated clipboard contents.

Local settings and files

Preferences such as language, appearance, shortcuts, drawing options, break timer settings, and recording options are stored locally with macOS UserDefaults. Drawings remain in memory until cleared or the tool closes. Saved recordings and images remain on your Mac until you remove them. You control any later copying, sharing, backup, or synchronization of these files and clipboard contents through macOS or other apps.

Children

Lumen does not collect personal information from users, including children.

Support email

If you contact support by email, the information and attachments you choose to send are shared through your email service for handling that support request. Lumen does not automatically attach recordings, images, or diagnostics.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published at this address with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, email emrecan.ayar@icloud.com.
