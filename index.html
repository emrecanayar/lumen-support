Lumen Support

Set up Lumen and get help.

Start here

Lumen is a menu-bar app for presentations and demos on macOS 14 or later. It includes Zoom, Draw, Laser pointer, Break timer, Live zoom, Record, and Snip. Click the menu-bar icon and choose Show Lumen to open its settings. Closing the main window leaves the tools available from the menu bar.

Screen Recording permission

Zoom, Snip, and Record need Screen Recording permission. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording (called Screen & System Audio Recording on some macOS versions) and enable Lumen. If a permission change does not take effect, quit and reopen Lumen.

Record the screen and audio

Choose the full screen, a selected area, or a window. System audio and microphone recording are optional. Turn on the audio sources you want before recording. The microphone is off by default; macOS requests microphone access when you enable it. Manage that permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Recordings are H.264 MP4 files saved in Movies/Lumen in your home folder. The recording bar lets you pause, draw, start over, delete the take, or stop. Click highlighting requires macOS 15 or later.

Capture and annotate a snip

Capture an area, a window, or the full screen. Add arrows, shapes, text, blur, or numbered steps. Copy the result to the clipboard, save a PNG in Pictures/Lumen, pin it on the screen, or copy text recognized locally with Apple’s Vision framework.

Click a shortcut badge in the main window to change it. Use Control, Option, or Command as a modifier. Press Esc to cancel. Esc also closes an active tool.

Languages and appearance

Lumen supports English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, and Italian. Choose a language and a light, dark, or system appearance in the main window.
